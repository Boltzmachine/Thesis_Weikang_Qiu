\partwithintro[part:bridge]{The Bridge: From Signals to Representations}{%
In Part~\ref{part:foundation}, we focused on modeling brain signals within the neural modality itself. In this part, we move beyond intra-modal modeling and investigate how to learn representations of brain activity that align with world-level semantics. While multimodal alignment has achieved remarkable progress in domains such as vision and language, brain signals remain substantially underexplored. Compared to image–text datasets, brain datasets are scarce, expensive to acquire, and highly variable across subjects. Therefore, building a reliable bridge from brain activity to semantic representations requires not only architectural designs grounded in cognitive principles but also carefully constructed datasets and proposed training paradigms that can operate under limited supervision and cross-subject variability}
