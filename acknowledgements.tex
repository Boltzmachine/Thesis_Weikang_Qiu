\frontmatterheading{Acknowledgments}

Many people contributed to the work in this dissertation and to the life I have had while writing it. I am grateful for the guidance, conversations, and care that accompanied my years at Yale.

First, I thank my advisor, Rex Ying, for his guidance and support throughout my PhD. I especially appreciate his respect for students' individual choices and development. He gave me room to pursue my interests and make decisions about my research and future. His responsiveness was equally important to me: I valued being able to bring him questions and ideas and receive timely advice. I appreciate the practical value of receiving an answer and being able to move forward, as well as the attention behind it. His respect for my independence and his readiness to respond were both important parts of his mentorship.

I also thank my committee members, Smita Krishnaswamy, Arman Cohan, and Yujun Yan (Dartmouth College), for their time and valuable feedback. I appreciate the attention they gave to my work and the opportunity to consider it from perspectives beyond my own.

I am grateful to my labmates Tinglin Huang, Jialin Chen, and Yangtian Zhang for the many conversations about ideas and research. We exchanged thoughts whether or not we were working on the same paper, and I valued their willingness to take an interest in questions outside our formal collaborations. These conversations gave me opportunities to try out an idea, discuss a difficulty, and hear how someone else understood a problem. I am thankful for the time and attention they gave so freely, and for the openness that made these exchanges possible. I also thank my external collaborators Zheng Huang, Aosong Feng, and Huashuo Lei. I am grateful for the ideas and perspectives they brought to our joint work, and for the time and effort they devoted to it.

Outside academia, I am grateful for the friendships that shaped my life in New Haven. I appreciated the ways my labmates and I looked after one another and the sense of familiarity that came from sharing the same city. I also thank the friends with whom I regularly shared drinks and conversations about literature, philosophy, and whatever else caught our interest. I enjoyed having people with whom to follow a thought, hear a different interpretation, and move freely between subjects. I am grateful for their company and for making room in our lives for these shared interests. I am also grateful for aespa, whose music has been a source of enjoyment throughout my doctoral studies.

Finally, I thank my parents for their unconditional love and support. I am grateful for the care they have given me throughout my life and for the many ways they have continued to support me as I pursued this work. This dissertation marks the completion of one part of my education; my gratitude to them reaches much further.
