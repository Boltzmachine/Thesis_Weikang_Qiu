\chapter{\mindllm{}: A Subject-Agnostic and Versatile Model for fMRI-to-Text Decoding}
\label{chapter:mindllm}

In Chapter~\ref{chapter:hybrid}, \hybrid{} identified relationships among brain regions that are informative for a specified cognitive outcome. We now ask how brain activity can reveal the semantic content of a person's visual experience and support different queries about that content. This requires a representation that accommodates individual differences while retaining information for multiple decoding tasks. This chapter introduces \mindllm{}, which combines a subject-agnostic fMRI encoder with a language model. Brain Instruction Tuning connects neural recordings to diverse text annotations through shared stimulus images, extending the focus from predicting a predefined outcome to flexible semantic readout.
