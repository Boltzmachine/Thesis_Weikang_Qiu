\section{Related Works}

\paragraph{EEG Foundation Models.}
Recent studies have explored self-supervised pretraining on large-scale unlabeled EEG data to learn transferable neural representations. BENDR~\citep{kostas2021bendr} employs contrastive learning, while MAEEG~\citep{chien2022maeeg} reconstructs masked EEG signals. LaBraM~\citep{jiang2024large} introduces neural token prediction over EEG patches, and EEGPT~\citep{wang2024eegpt} combines multiple self-supervised objectives for general EEG representation learning. Recent works have also investigated different model architectures: CBraMod~\citep{wang2025cbramod} employs criss-cross attention, EEGMamba~\citep{wang2025eegmamba} adopts state-space modeling, and LUNA~\citep{doner2025luna} uses learnable queries to support different electrode layouts. Beyond unimodal representation learning, NeuroLM~\citep{jiang2025neurolm} connects EEG with language, BrainOmni~\citep{xiao2025brainomni} jointly models EEG and MEG, and REVE~\citep{ouahidi2025reve} explores large-scale pretraining on EEG recordings from 25,000 subjects.


\paragraph{World Models.}
World models learn environment dynamics from experience and support decision-making by predicting the consequences of actions~\citep{ha2018recurrent}. Generative methods directly predict future observations: IRIS~\citep{micheli2023transformers} autoregressively models discrete image tokens, DIAMOND~\citep{alonso2024diffusion} adopts diffusion-based video prediction, and Genie~\citep{bruce2024genie} learns interactive environments with latent actions. Latent-dynamics methods instead support control in compact representation spaces; Dreamer~\citep{hafner2020dream} trains policies from imagined trajectories, while TD-MPC2~\citep{hansen2024tdmpc2} performs latent model-predictive control. Recent joint-embedding approaches shift prediction from pixels to latent representations. DINO-WM~\citep{zhou2025dinowm} predicts future pretrained visual features for zero-shot planning, whereas PLDM~\citep{sobal2025rewardfree} jointly learns visual representations and latent dynamics. V-JEPA~2 scales masked latent prediction to web-scale video, and its action-conditioned variant, V-JEPA~2-AC, is post-trained over a frozen encoder for image-goal robotic planning~\citep{assran2025vjepa2}. LeJEPA introduces Sketched Isotropic Gaussian Regularization for stable joint-embedding learning~\citep{balestriero2025lejepa}. LeWorldModel applies this regularization to action-conditioned latent dynamics, jointly training an encoder and predictor from raw pixels for latent-space model-predictive control~\citep{maes2026leworldmodel}.
